# Landing page (app/page.tsx)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: prospective members (Edinburgh students) and sponsors. Primary action: email (mailto), always in the first viewport and repeated per team and in the footer. Proof: real build/meeting photos only; weight-class limits are rules, not claims. No invented results, sponsors, stats.

Content lives in `content/site.ts` (links, copy, photos, committee); the page renders entirely from it. Beetleweight has 3 sub-teams (weapons, chassis, drive). Missing photos render as hatched "PHOTO TO ADD" placeholders.

Unresolved: real email, Instagram, Linktree, Discord URLs; committee names/photos; build photos.
