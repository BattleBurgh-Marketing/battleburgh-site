## What is Battleburgh

Battleburgh is a newly founded society at the university of Edinburgh focusing on battle bots and the unibot competition.

This year, we compete in the following categories:
1. Antweight bot (4 people a team)
2. Beetleweight bot
3. Unibot

### Our committee:

President, Vice President, Secretary, Treasurer

Antweight bot lead

Control lead (unibots)

Beetleweight:
Weapons lead
Chassis lead
Drive lead

Social Media lead

### What we have achieved
As a new society, we managed to build a robot from scratch in a semester
